About me

I’m a data engineer and the founder of Calamity Technology Solutions Group (CTSG). Today I help Houston home service companies turn the customer data they already have into booked jobs, and I measure every campaign against a holdout group so the results are real.

The skills behind that come from 7+ years of building data platforms on regulated, multi-system data: pipelines that pull many systems into one clean database, reporting that leaders actually use, and the T-SQL and Python fundamentals that decide whether the layer on top holds up under load.

Most recently I led the AI-driven modernization of an enterprise data platform on Azure Data Factory, Copilot Studio, Azure AI Foundry, and n8n — setting technical direction for the team, quantifying the monthly cost savings, and presenting those outcomes directly to leadership. Along the way I architected a schema-drift framework that self-adapts during ingestion, which ended a category of recurring production incidents outright.

I have also built and run my own businesses across professional services and e-commerce, so ROI-driven decisions and the cost of getting it wrong are not abstractions to me. I work directly with the owner, prove things on real data before anyone commits to a full build, and own the work from the first conversation through delivery. There is no separate delivery team I pass it to.

Outside of client work I create A.I Build with AJ on YouTube, sharing practical guidance on AI, agents, and data engineering. I also build browser games as a way to keep shipping finished, polished things end to end.

The standard I hold the work to is the one on my front page: do it right the first time, you won’t have to do it again.
