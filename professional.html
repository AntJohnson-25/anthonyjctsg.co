About Me

I am an AI-focused data leader and business-minded technologist with 7+ years spent turning strategic objectives into data and AI solutions that actually move the number — revenue up or cost down. I bring the P&L awareness of someone who has run their own ventures and the technical depth to architect the platform underneath.

That combination is the whole point. I have built and operated businesses across professional services and e-commerce, so stakeholder management, ROI-driven decisions, and the cost of getting it wrong are not abstractions to me. I bring that instinct to the architecture work: cloud data platforms and AI-orchestrated workflows, designed end to end.

Most recently I led the AI-driven modernization of an enterprise data platform on Azure Data Factory, Copilot Studio, Azure AI Foundry, and n8n — setting technical direction for the team, quantifying the monthly cost savings, and presenting those outcomes directly to leadership to secure continued investment in AI. Along the way I architected an intelligent schema-drift framework that self-adapts during ingestion, which ended a category of recurring production incidents outright.

The work I do best happens embedded alongside client teams inside their own environments. I scope ambiguous requirements on the fly, prototype against real data to prove feasibility before anyone commits to a full build, and iterate on live feedback instead of waiting on handoff cycles. I own the solution from the pre-sales conversation through production deployment — there is no separate delivery team I pass it to.

I translate in both directions. I gather requirements from business stakeholders and explain engineering trade-offs in plain business language, across healthcare, insurance, and manufacturing. Underneath the AI work sits a deep foundation in T-SQL performance tuning, ETL/ELT design, and Python — the unglamorous fundamentals that decide whether the impressive layer on top holds up under load.

Outside of client work I mentor engineers, evangelize responsible AI adoption, and create A.I Build with AJ on YouTube, where I share practical guidance on AI, agents, and modern data engineering. I also build browser games as a way to keep shipping finished, polished things end to end — you can play them in the portfolio.

I am a continuous learner who leads by staying hands-on, solving problems as part of the team rather than from the sidelines. Strong ownership, sharp business intuition, and a relentless pursuit of excellence — held to the standard on my front page: do it right the first time, you won’t have to do it again.
